About United Transport Services

A legacy of trust built over five decades — moving Pakistan forward since 1970.

Our Story

Established 1970 — Moving Pakistan for Over 55 Years

United Transport Services began in 1970 with a single vision: to provide Pakistan with a transport service defined by reliability, safety and uncompromising professionalism. What started as a modest operation has grown into one of the nation's most respected transport companies, with a fleet exceeding 500 vehicles and a presence across 9 strategic locations including Saudi Arabia.

Over five decades, we have moved corporate executives, international delegations, tourists, students and families — earning the trust of multinational organisations, government bodies and private clients alike. Our commitment to maintenance excellence, trained chauffeurs and 24/7 support has made UTS the partner of choice for mission-critical mobility.

Today, UTS continues to innovate — integrating modern fleet management, GPS tracking and dedicated service programs while never losing sight of the values that built our reputation.

Our Mission

To deliver safe, punctual and premium transport solutions that exceed expectations, while setting the benchmark for professionalism in Pakistan's mobility sector.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and technologically advanced transport company in the region, connecting people and businesses with seamless mobility.

Core Values

Safety first, integrity always, relentless reliability, customer obsession and continuous improvement — the principles guiding every journey.
