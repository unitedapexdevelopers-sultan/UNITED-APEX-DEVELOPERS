Process

How we build your app.

A clear, phased process — so you always know what's being built, why, and when it ships.

01
Discovery & Scope
We learn your business and users, define the core features that matter first, and agree a phased build plan.
02
Design
We design the user experience and screens before writing code, so you approve the product before it's built.
03
Build & Test
We build in phases with regular check-ins, testing continuously rather than all at once at the end.
04
Launch + Support
We handle store submission and deployment, then stay on for support and the next phase of features.
