Case Study · Transport & Mobility

UTS Karlo — one app to rent, ride, track and run a transport company.

We built a single mobile app for United Transport Services: customers rent a car with a driver or book pick & drop in a few taps, staff and managers run bookings and the fleet from a protected portal, and live vehicle tracking stays secure on our own server.

The Challenge

Decades of experience — but no digital front door.

UTS began in 1970 in Karachi as a family-run rent-a-car operation and grew into a transport company serving Pakistan's major cities and Saudi Arabia. Customers phoned or messaged, staff checked availability by hand, and the information needed to run the business lived in different places.

01
Bookings by phone and message
Every rental started with a call or a chat — there was no self-service way to see which cars were free and book one.
02
Multi-day pricing done by hand
Rates differ by car, model year, local or outstation trip and overnight stay — and had to be multiplied out for every booking.
03
A mixed fleet
UTS-owned cars sit alongside vendor and partner vehicles, each needing approval, pricing and a clear free/busy status.
04
Tracking data that had to stay private
The GPS tracker's access key could never sit inside an app — yet managers needed the whole fleet on a map, and customers sometimes needed one vehicle.
05
Different people, different access
Customers, front-desk staff and managers each needed their own view — with sensitive tools hidden from everyone else.
06
Students and institutions
Recurring student transport needed a request-and-approval flow before any vehicle or location details are shared.
The Solution

Three connected pieces, one real-time database.

A booking made on a phone appears in the staff inbox the moment it is sent — and the whole UTS fleet is offered in every city, because cars are dispatched to the customer.

Rent a Car

From "is a car free?" to booked — without a phone call.

Pick a city and dates, see only the cars free for the whole trip, choose a model year, and see the full price before booking.

One card per model, with model years
"Toyota Corolla" opens into its model-year options; the photo, specs and price switch with the year chosen. Special trims such as the Fortuner Legender get their own option.
Automatic multi-day pricing
The app multiplies the daily rate by the trip length and shows the working — for example PKR 12,000/day × 5 days = PKR 60,000 — with local, outstation and overnight rates applied automatically.
Free for the whole trip
A car only appears if it is free on every day of the requested range, and staff can mark any car Busy in one tap.
Verified and rewarded
Customers add their CNIC and an ID photo for verification, can use loyalty credits, and the receipt shows the full breakdown.

Staff & Manager Portal

Run the business from the same app.

SubmissionsRent a car, pick & drop, corporate and career requests in one inbox, each with its status.
Fleet & partnersAll cars with search and free/busy; add UTS-owned or vendor cars; approve, price or remove partner vehicles.
Manager toolsStats and partner revenue, drivers, employees, and a full log of every admin action.
Hidden by designCustomers never see the portal; it opens with a hidden gesture and a passcode, and manager tools need a second one.
Secure Live Tracking

The tracker's key never leaves our server.

The app talks to our own tracking server, which checks who is asking before it ever contacts the GPS provider — so nothing sensitive can leak from a phone.

Whole fleet for managers
Every tracked vehicle on a live street map — moving, engine on, parked or offline — with search by plate. Built for 262 vehicles.
Links that expire
Staff pick a plate and a number of hours; the server signs a pass that stops working on its own.
Nothing to steal
No tracker key in the app and no fleet data for customers — a shared link reveals one vehicle and nothing else.
Brand & Engagement

An app people open again.

UTS's own look, a home screen that leads with what customers book most, light and dark mode, and nine games with live leaderboards.

The Result

One branded app — for customers and the team.

Customers can see what's free, pick the exact model year, know the full price up front and follow their vehicle. The team has every request in one inbox, the fleet's status at a glance and a record of every admin action.
