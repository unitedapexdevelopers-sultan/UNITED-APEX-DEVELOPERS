Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

United Apex Developers ("we," "us," "our") operates unitedapexdevelopers.com. This page explains what information we collect when you use our website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide directly: name, email address, phone number (optional), the service you're interested in, and your message. This is processed through Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, and delivered to our email inbox.

If Google Analytics is active on this site, we also automatically collect standard usage data — pages visited, approximate location (city/country level), device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry and discuss the services you're interested in
  • To understand which pages and services visitors find most useful, so we can improve the site
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Formspree — processes and delivers contact form submissions
  • Google Analytics (GA4), where enabled — collects anonymized site usage data
  • Google Fonts — loads typefaces used on this site

Cookies

This site may use cookies set by Google Analytics to distinguish returning visitors from new ones. These cookies do not contain personally identifying information. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained in our email inbox for as long as needed to respond to and follow up on your inquiry, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your Rights

Depending on where you're located (including under UK GDPR and EU GDPR, given we serve clients in the UK and Europe), you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, email us at unitedapexdevelopers@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to unitedapexdevelopers@gmail.com.

This policy is a general template intended to accurately describe our current data practices. It is not a substitute for legal advice. Given United Apex Developers serves clients in the UK and EU (where GDPR applies) and other markets with their own privacy laws, we recommend having this reviewed by a qualified professional to confirm full compliance for your specific jurisdictions before relying on it as a legal document.
